# Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

## Khatario kya hai?

Khatario Indian dukaan aur business ke liye billing software hai. Isme GST bill (invoice) banana, stock (maal) ka hisaab, grahak aur supplier, udhaar aur payment, GST report, WhatsApp par bill bhejna aur online dukaan, sab ek hi app mein hai. Staff ki hazri (attendance) aur salary ke liye Khatario HR jaldi aa raha hai (coming soon); abhi yeh available nahi hai.

## Kya Khatario free hai?

Haan. Free plan hamesha ke liye free hai, bas mahine mein invoice, customer aur item ki limit hai. Naye account ko 30 din ka free trial milta hai jisme poora product chalta hai, aur card ki zarurat nahi hai. Paid plan ki kimat plans and pricing document mein likhi hai.

## Bill kaise banaye? Kitna time lagta hai?

Customer chuno, item daalo (naam se dhundho ya barcode scan karo), aur Khatario price, HSN code aur GST khud bhar deta hai. Ek bill mein ek minute se kam lagta hai. Phir print karo, PDF download karo ya WhatsApp par bhej do.

## Kya bill GST wala (pakka bill) hota hai?

Haan. Har bill mein HSN/SAC code hota hai aur CGST, SGST ya IGST place of supply ke hisaab se apne aap lagta hai. Bina GST wale business bill of supply bana sakte hain.

## GST return (GSTR-1) kaise bhare?

Khatario aapke bills se GSTR-1 aur GSTR-3B taiyaar karta hai. Inhe Excel ya JSON mein export karke GST portal par upload karo ya apne CA ko bhej do. Khatario seedha portal par return file nahi karta. GST report Business aur Enterprise plan mein aur trial mein milti hai.

## Udhaar (baaki paisa) ka hisaab kaise rakhe?

Har customer ka baaki paisa, kitne din se baaki hai (ageing) aur statement Khatario mein dikhta hai. WhatsApp par payment reminder ek-ek karke ya sabko ek saath bhej sakte ho, aur payment link se customer UPI se turant pay kar sakta hai.

## Stock (maal) ka hisaab apne aap hota hai?

Haan. Har bikri par stock kam hota hai aur har kharid (purchase) par badhta hai. Stock khatam hone se pehle low stock alert milta hai. Alag-alag godown (warehouse) ka stock bhi alag dikhta hai.

## Internet nahi hai to kya bill ban sakta hai?

Haan. Bina internet ke bhi billing chalti rehti hai. Bill phone ya computer par save ho jata hai aur internet aate hi apne aap sync ho jata hai.

## Mobile se chala sakte hain?

Haan. Khatario phone, tablet aur computer ke browser mein chalta hai, aur Android app bhi hai. Sab device par ek hi data dikhta hai. iPhone ke liye alag app nahi hai, Safari mein khol kar home screen par add kar lo.

## Printer kaunsa chalega?

58mm aur 80mm Bluetooth thermal printer se seedha receipt print hoti hai. Normal printer se A4 PDF bill print kar sakte ho.

## WhatsApp par bill kaise bheje?

Kisi bhi invoice par WhatsApp button dabao, customer ko bill ka PDF ya link chala jata hai, saath mein payment link bhi bhej sakte ho. Apna WhatsApp number QR code scan karke jodna hota hai.

## Staff ko bhi login de sakte hain?

Haan. Paid plan mein kai staff users ban sakte hain, har ek ka alag login aur permission. Jaise billing wala staff bill bana sake par profit report na dekh sake. Kitne users milenge, yeh plan par depend karta hai.

## Kya mera data safe hai?

Haan. Sab data encrypted HTTPS se jata hai, staff ko sirf wahi dikhta hai jo aap allow karo, aur aapka data aapka hai, Khatario use advertisers ko nahi bechta. Settings mein backup aur restore ka option hai.

## Tally ya Excel se Khatario par kaise aaye?

Zyada tar dukaan pehle din se billing shuru kar deti hain. Item list CSV file se import karo, opening balance daalo, aur customer bill banate waqt jodte jao. Madad chahiye to free demo book karo.

## Demo kaise book kare?

khatario.com/book-demo par date aur time chuno, naam, email aur mobile number daalo aur WhatsApp code se verify karo. Ya is chat mein "demo book karna hai" likho, assistant book kar dega.

## Trial khatam hone ke baad kya hoga?

Trial khatam hone par aap 7 din ka extension ek baar le sakte ho, paid plan le sakte ho, ya Free plan par chal sakte ho. Aapka data account mein rehta hai.
