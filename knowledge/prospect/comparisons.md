# Why switch from manual billing or Excel?

## How is Khatario better than handwritten bills?

Handwritten bills are slow at closing time, easy to get wrong on GST, and give you no running record of who owes what. With Khatario one bill takes under a minute, GST is calculated for you, stock updates automatically and every unpaid bill shows up in receivables.

## How is Khatario better than Excel or spreadsheets?

Spreadsheets need manual formulas for GST, break when several people edit them, and do not print proper GST bills or produce GSTR-1. Khatario keeps bills, stock, customers, payments and GST reports in one place, with separate logins and permissions for staff.

## How does Khatario compare with other billing software?

Khatario focuses on the full daily flow for Indian shops in one app: GST billing, stock, credit and WhatsApp collections, GST reports, an online store and HR, working on phone and computer and offline at the counter. It has a free plan and a free trial with no card needed, so the best comparison is to try it on your own data or book a demo. Be aware of what Khatario does not do yet (for example e-invoicing and Tally export), listed in "What Khatario does not do yet".

## What problems does Khatario solve?

- Slow manual billing: one bill in under a minute.
- Fear of GST mistakes: built-in tax logic and clean GST exports.
- Chasing payments: WhatsApp reminders and a clear list of who owes you.
- Stock mismatch: stock moves with every bill, with low-stock alerts.
