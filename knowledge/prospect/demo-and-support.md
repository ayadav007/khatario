# Book a demo and get support

## How do I book a demo?

Book a free live demo at khatario.com/book-demo, or ask the assistant to book it for you. Choose a date (from tomorrow up to 60 days ahead) and an available time slot, enter your name, email and mobile number, and verify your number with the WhatsApp code. You get a booking number, and the Khatario team walks you through the product on a call, on your own data if you like.

## What happens in the demo?

A Khatario team member shows how billing, stock, GST reports and WhatsApp would work for your business, answers your questions, and helps you choose a plan and set up your account.

## How do I contact Khatario support?

Contact details and support hours are in the "Contact Khatario support" document. You can also book a demo to talk to a real person, and read guides and how-tos at khatario.com/guides.

## Will a real person help me?

Yes. Real people on the Khatario team handle demos and support. The assistant can pass your details to the team and book a call.
