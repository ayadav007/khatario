# Who is Khatario for?

## Is Khatario good for retail shops and kirana stores?

Yes. Retail shops get fast counter billing with barcode scanning, POS mode with Bluetooth thermal printing, stock that updates with every sale, low-stock alerts and WhatsApp bills. Billing keeps working offline.

## Is Khatario good for wholesalers and distributors?

Yes. Wholesalers and distributors get party-wise credit tracking and credit limits, customer statements, WhatsApp payment reminders, multiple warehouses, batches and expiry, and receivables ageing to see who has owed money the longest.

## Is Khatario good for manufacturers?

Yes. Manufacturers can manage purchases of raw material, work orders, delivery challans, multiple warehouses, stock valuation and GST reports.

## Is Khatario good for service businesses?

Yes. Service businesses can bill services with SAC codes, send quotations and estimates, use recurring invoices for monthly clients and track payments.

## Is Khatario good for restaurants?

Khatario can handle restaurant billing and stock, but it does not have kitchen order tickets (KOT) or table management. Restaurants that need KOT should book a demo to discuss.

## Which industries use Khatario?

Businesses in pharmaceuticals, textiles and garments, electronics, food and beverages, automotive, construction, FMCG distribution and services, among others.

## Is Khatario only for GST-registered businesses?

No. GST-registered businesses get full GST invoicing and returns, and businesses that are not registered or use the composition scheme can issue bills of supply.
