# Switching to Khatario

## I use Tally, Vyapar or a notebook today. How hard is it to switch?

Most shops start billing on day one. Import your item list from a CSV file (Khatario gives you the template), add customers as they come in, enter opening balances for customers, suppliers and accounts, and keep your old books for past years. If you want help, book a free demo and the team will walk through it on your data.

## Can I import my existing data?

- Items: yes, from a CSV file using the template.
- Opening balances: yes, entered in Opening Balances when you start mid-year.
- Bank statements: yes, CSV or PDF for reconciliation.
- GSTR-2B: yes, for purchase reconciliation.
- WhatsApp contacts: yes, from CSV.
- Customers: add them from the Customers screen or while billing; there is no bulk customer import file today.

## Can Khatario export to Tally?

No. Khatario does not export data in Tally format. It has a Tally-style invoice layout, and its own complete accounting and GST reports, so most businesses do not need Tally alongside it. Reports can be exported to Excel for your CA.

## How long does setup take?

Basic setup (business profile, GSTIN, logo, bank details and invoice number series) takes a few minutes. Then add or import items and start billing.
