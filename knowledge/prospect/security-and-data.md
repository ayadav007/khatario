# Is my data safe with Khatario?

## How does Khatario keep my business data safe?

- Everything travels over encrypted HTTPS between your device and Khatario.
- Sign-in is required, and role-based access means staff only see the modules and branches you allow.
- Payment gateway keys you connect are stored encrypted.
- Closed periods can be locked so past bills cannot be changed.

## Who owns my data?

You do. Your customer list, bills and business data are yours, and Khatario does not sell them to advertisers. The privacy policy at khatario.com/privacy has the details.

## Does Khatario back up my data?

Khatario has a Backup & restore section in Settings where you can take a backup at any time, schedule automatic backups, connect Google Drive, and restore a backup (replacing everything or merging). Which backup options your plan includes is shown in the plans document.

## Can I export my data?

Yes. Take a full backup from Settings > Backup & restore, and export reports and GST returns as Excel or JSON.

## What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Your account moves to the Free plan and your data stays in it. You can upgrade again later and continue where you left off.
