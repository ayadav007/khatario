# How Khatario pricing works

## Is there a free plan?

Yes. Khatario Billing has a Free plan that is free forever, meant for trying Khatario properly and for very small businesses. It has monthly limits on invoices, customers and items; the exact limits are in the plans and pricing document. Khatario Connect is also free, with paid WhatsApp add-ons.

## Which plans are available?

- Billing: Free / Starter, Professional, Business and Enterprise
- Connect: free platform with WhatsApp Bot and Send Message add-ons

Khatario HR is coming soon, so there are no HR plans or prices yet.

The exact price, limits and features of each plan are listed in the "Khatario plans and pricing" document, which is taken live from Khatario's plan settings.

## Can I pay yearly instead of monthly?

Yes. Every paid plan can be paid monthly or yearly, and the yearly price works out cheaper than twelve monthly payments. The exact yearly prices and savings are in the plans document.

## How do I pay for a plan?

Upgrade from Settings > Plan & billing inside Khatario and pay online through Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking).

## Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes. You can change your plan from Settings > Plan & billing at any time. If you reach a plan limit (for example the monthly invoice limit), Khatario shows you before you hit it and suggests the plan that fits.

## What happens if my paid plan expires?

If a paid plan is not renewed, there is a 7-day grace period, after which the account moves to the Free plan. Your data stays in your account.

## Which plan is right for me?

It depends on how many bills you make, how many staff and branches you have, and whether you need GST reports, WhatsApp automation or an online store. Ask the assistant to recommend a plan, or book a free demo and the team will help you choose.
