# Purchases, expenses and suppliers

## Can I record supplier bills in Khatario?

Yes. Enter purchase bills with bill number, date, items, GST and payment status. Finalising a purchase adds the stock to your warehouse and records what you owe the supplier. Purchase documents include tax invoice, bill of supply and bill of entry (for imports), with reverse charge where it applies.

## Can Khatario read a supplier bill from a photo?

Yes. Upload a photo or PDF of a supplier bill and Khatario's AI reads the supplier, bill details and items for you. Always review the extracted details before saving.

## Does Khatario handle purchase orders and returns?

Yes. Create purchase orders for suppliers, convert them into purchases when goods arrive, and record purchase returns that reduce stock and reverse the tax.

## Can I track business expenses?

Yes. Record expenses such as rent, electricity or transport by category, with GST where applicable, either paid immediately or as a bill to pay later.

## Can I see how much I owe each supplier?

Yes. Supplier balances, payables ageing and supplier statements show exactly what is due and to whom. Record payments made to suppliers to clear the balance.
