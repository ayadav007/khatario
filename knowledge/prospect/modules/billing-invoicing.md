# GST billing and invoicing in Khatario

## How fast can I make a bill?

Most bills take under a minute: pick the customer, add items (search by name or scan a barcode), and Khatario fills in price, HSN/SAC code and GST rate from the item. Save and the bill is ready to print, download as PDF or share on WhatsApp.

## Are Khatario bills GST compliant?

Yes. Every bill carries HSN/SAC codes and Khatario works out CGST, SGST or IGST from the place of supply. Bills show your GSTIN, the customer's GSTIN, tax-wise totals and round-off, in a format your CA will recognise.

## Which document types can I create?

- Tax invoice
- Bill of supply (for exempt or composition sales)
- Proforma invoice, which can later be converted into a tax invoice
- Estimates and quotations
- Sales orders
- Delivery challans
- Credit notes and debit notes
- Work orders
- Recurring invoices that repeat automatically on a schedule
- Export invoices with shipping bill, port, currency and incoterms fields

Which of these you can use depends on your plan; see the plans and pricing document.

## Can I record e-way bill details on an invoice?

You can store and print the e-way bill number and date on an invoice or delivery challan, and download the e-way bill JSON from a challan for the portal's bulk upload. Khatario does not generate e-way bills or e-invoices (IRN) with the government portal itself.

## Can I customise how my invoice looks?

Yes. Choose from invoice templates (GST standard, modern, Tally-style layout, export, bill of supply, delivery challan and 58mm/80mm thermal receipts) and customise your logo, colours, fields, bank details, terms and signature. Custom fields can be added to items and invoices.

## How do I share an invoice with my customer?

Every invoice can be downloaded as a PDF, printed, or shared on WhatsApp. Khatario also creates a secure public link for each invoice so the customer can open and download it without an account, and you can include a payment link so they can pay online.

## Can I take payment on the bill itself?

Yes. When the customer pays at the counter you record the amount and mode (cash, UPI, card, bank) on the bill. Unpaid bills show up in receivables so you can follow up.

## Is there a POS or counter billing mode?

Yes. POS mode gives a fast counter layout with parked bills and automatic printing to a Bluetooth thermal printer, which suits retail counters.

## Can I give different customers different prices?

Yes, from the Starter plan upwards. Set a customer's own price per item (or per size and colour), put customers on price levels such as Wholesale or Dealer with quantity slabs, add customer and category discounts, and run schemes like "buy 10, get 1 free". Bills pick the right price automatically, never above MRP, and show the last rate charged and a warning when you sell below cost.

## Can I correct a final invoice or give a discount after the sale?

Yes. A final invoice can be amended in the full editor until the GSTR-1 that includes it is filed. A discount given after the sale (a turnover or year-end discount) is a post-sale discount credit note covering a supply period.

## Can I print bills in Hindi or other Indian languages?

Yes. Invoice headings print in English, Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Punjabi, Odia or Urdu. There is also a Distributor (FMCG / Pharma) template with cases and pieces, MRP and retailer margin columns.
