# WhatsApp in Khatario

## Can I send invoices on WhatsApp?

Yes. Share any invoice on WhatsApp in one tap. The customer gets the invoice PDF or a secure link, and you can include a payment link. Invoice sharing is part of the billing plans; the daily WhatsApp message limit depends on your plan.

## How do I connect my WhatsApp number?

Scan a QR code with WhatsApp on your phone, the same way you use WhatsApp Web, and Khatario can send messages from your number. Businesses that use the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API from Meta can connect it with their own credentials instead.

## What is the WhatsApp CRM inbox?

The conversations inbox shows all customer chats in one place for your team, with labels, notes, custom fields, a timeline, linked orders and export. Several team members can work the same inbox.

## What can the WhatsApp bot do?

The WhatsApp Bot add-on answers customers automatically with rules and auto-replies, can take product enquiries and orders on WhatsApp (you approve or reject each order), and labels conversations. An AI assistant can reply using your product list, prices and policies.

## Can I send bulk messages or campaigns?

Yes. Import contacts from CSV, create contact groups and send campaigns with text, images, documents and buttons, with scheduling and anti-ban pacing. Only message customers who have agreed to hear from you; unsubscribes are tracked.

## What is Khatario Connect?

Khatario Connect is the WhatsApp-only product: a free account with no monthly platform fee, where you switch on the WhatsApp Bot add-on and/or the Send Message add-on as monthly add-ons. You can add Billing later from the same account. Add-on prices are listed in the plans and pricing document.

## Does Khatario send payment reminders on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send reminders to customers with outstanding balances one by one, and with the WhatsApp Bot add-on send them in bulk or schedule them to go out automatically. Delivery status and errors are logged.
