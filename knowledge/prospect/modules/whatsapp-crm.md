# WhatsApp in Khatario

Khatario has two ways to use WhatsApp:

- **QR link (every plan, including Free):** scan a QR code with WhatsApp on your phone, the same way you use WhatsApp Web, and Khatario sends from your own number.
- **Khatario Connect (paid plan):** connect your own WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Cloud API, and get the team inbox, AI agent, templates and automation.

## Can I send invoices on WhatsApp?

Yes, on every plan. Share any invoice or other document on WhatsApp in one tap from a QR-linked number. The customer gets the PDF or a secure link, and you can include a payment link. How many WhatsApp messages you can send per day depends on your plan.

## How do I connect my WhatsApp number?

Scan a QR code with WhatsApp on your phone from Settings > WhatsApp. This works on every plan, including Free. To use the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API from Meta with your own WhatsApp Business Account, you need the paid Connect plan; you then enter your Meta credentials in Khatario.

## What are the limits of the QR link?

The QR link uses your normal WhatsApp number, so Khatario spaces messages out to lower the risk of WhatsApp blocking the number, and there is a daily message limit set by your plan. Your phone's WhatsApp session must stay linked; if it disconnects, scan the QR code again. With the QR link you send invoices, documents and payment reminders. Custom messages, campaigns, the shared inbox, the bot and the AI agent need Connect.

## Does Khatario send payment reminders on WhatsApp?

Yes. On every plan you can send a payment reminder to a customer from a QR-linked number. From the Starter plan upwards you can also send reminders for many invoices at once.

Automatic reminders (before the due date, and repeated while an invoice is overdue) are included from the **Starter** billing plan upwards. They go out on their own from your QR-linked number; you do not need Connect for them. If you also have Connect with the official API set up, they go out through the official API instead. The Free plan does not include automatic reminders, so reminders there are sent by hand.

Delivery status and errors are logged.

## What is Khatario Connect?

Khatario Connect is the paid WhatsApp plan. It adds:

- the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API on your own WhatsApp Business Account
- a shared team inbox with labels, notes, custom fields, a timeline, linked orders and export
- a sales pipeline with deal values and follow-up reminders for agents
- bot rules and auto-replies, and an AI agent that replies using your product list, prices and policies
- Meta-approved message templates, custom and bulk messages, and campaigns
- the WhatsApp shop, where customers can browse and order (you approve or reject each order)
- WhatsApp agent seats, separate from your billing users

You can open a free Connect account to link a number by QR, then buy the Connect plan when you want these features. Prices are in the plans and pricing document.

## Can I send bulk messages or campaigns?

Yes, with Connect. Import contacts from CSV, create contact groups and send campaigns with text, images, documents and buttons, with scheduling and pacing. Only message customers who have agreed to hear from you; unsubscribes are tracked. Bulk payment reminders are the exception: those come with the Starter billing plan and above, and do not need Connect.
