# GST reports and returns in Khatario

## Can I file GSTR-1 from Khatario?

Khatario prepares your GSTR-1 from the bills you make. You can export it as Excel, as the GST portal's offline-tool Excel format, or as JSON, and upload it to the GST portal yourself or send it to your CA. Khatario does not submit returns directly to the GST portal; after you file, you can mark the return as filed in Khatario, which locks that period so bills cannot be changed by mistake.

## Which GST reports does Khatario have?

- GSTR-1 (outward supplies / sales)
- GSTR-3B (monthly tax summary)
- GSTR-9 (annual return view)
- GSTR-2B import and GSTR-2B reconciliation, which matches your purchase bills against what suppliers reported
- GST reconciliation alerts
- Tax-wise sales and purchase reports

GST reports are included in the Business and Enterprise plans and during the free trial.

## How does GSTR-2B reconciliation help?

Import your GSTR-2B and Khatario compares it with the purchase bills you entered. It shows which bills match, which are missing on either side, and where amounts differ, so you claim the right input tax credit.

## Does Khatario help with HSN and SAC codes?

Yes. There is an HSN/SAC finder to look up codes and rates, validation of codes on items, and an AI check that flags HSN codes that look wrong for the item description.

## Does Khatario validate GSTIN numbers?

Yes. GSTIN format is validated when you add customers and suppliers, and there is a GSTIN lookup tool.

## Does Khatario support e-invoicing (IRN) or e-way bill generation?

No. Khatario does not generate e-invoices (IRN) or e-way bills with the government portals. You can store and print an e-way bill number on the invoice.
