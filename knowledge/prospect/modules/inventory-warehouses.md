# Inventory and stock in Khatario

## Does stock update automatically when I sell?

Yes. Stock goes down with every sale and up with every purchase bill, so the stock you see matches what you actually sold and bought. Low-stock alerts tell you what to reorder.

## Can I manage multiple warehouses or godowns?

Yes. Add warehouses (godowns) and branches, see stock per location, and move goods between them with stock transfers that include a receive step at the other end. You can control which staff can use which warehouse.

## Does Khatario support batches, expiry dates and serial numbers?

Yes. Items can track batch numbers with expiry dates or individual serial numbers, which helps pharmacies, FMCG distributors and electronics shops. Item variants (such as size and colour) are also supported.

## Which stock valuation methods are available?

Stock can be valued using FIFO, LIFO or weighted average cost. Reports include stock summary, stock valuation, closing stock, stock movement, low stock and expired stock.

## Can I print barcodes and labels?

Yes. Generate barcodes for items, print barcode labels, and scan barcodes with a scanner or your phone camera while billing.

## How do I correct stock after a physical count?

Use inventory adjustments to record recounts, damage or shrinkage with a reason, so your stock and valuation stay correct.

## Can my supplier see when I am running low?

If your supplier is also on Khatario and connected to you, Khatario can notify them when your stock drops below a threshold so a refill can start before you call. This works only between connected Khatario businesses.

## Can I import my item list?

Yes. Import items from a CSV file using the template Khatario provides.

## Can I add my existing stock by scanning barcodes?

Yes. Scan & Add Items (web and phone app, every plan) scans each barcode, tells you if the shop already has it, fills new products from product databases or by reading photos of the label, and saves them with opening stock, batch and expiry.

## Can I sell in boxes and pieces, or repack bulk stock?

Yes. Give an item alternate units (BOX = 12 PCS, BAG = 50 KG) and pick the unit on each bill line; stock is always kept in the base unit. Repack turns a bulk bag into your own packets in one step, with wastage recorded and the cost carried into the packets.

## Does Khatario handle manufacturing, held stock and goods in transit?

Yes. Bills of materials and production entries are costed through work in progress, with by-products and scrap. Stock can be put on hold (quarantine, damaged, returns awaiting inspection), others' goods in your godown are registered, purchases can be booked before the goods arrive, and returnable challans track stock out with job workers or on approval. Reports cover Form 3CD quantity details, the bank stock statement and ITC-04 job work.
