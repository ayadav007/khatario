# Mobile app, offline billing and printing

## Does Khatario work on my phone?

Yes. Khatario runs in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer, and you can add it to your home screen like an app. There is also an Android app. Your data is the same on every device, so you can bill at the counter and check reports from home.

## Is there an iPhone (iOS) app?

There is no separate iPhone app. On an iPhone or iPad, open Khatario in Safari and add it to the home screen; it works like an app from there.

## What happens if the internet goes down?

You can keep billing. Bills, items, customers and new purchases made offline are saved on the device and sync automatically when the connection comes back, so the counter queue does not stop. Some actions, such as finalising certain documents, need a connection.

## Which printers does Khatario support?

- 58mm and 80mm thermal receipt printers connected by Bluetooth, from the browser or the Android app (ESC/POS printers)
- Any regular printer for A4 PDF bills

USB and network (Wi-Fi/LAN) thermal printing is not available yet; print A4 or thermal PDFs through your computer's printer driver instead.

## Can I print automatically after each bill?

Yes. In POS mode, bills can print automatically to your Bluetooth thermal printer as soon as they are saved.
