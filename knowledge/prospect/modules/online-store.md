# Your own online store

## Can I sell online with Khatario?

Yes. Khatario can create an online store for your business at your own address, such as yourshop.khatario.com, using the items and prices already in Khatario. Customers can browse products, add to cart, check out and track their orders. The online store is part of the Business plan and is available to try during the free trial.

## How do customers pay on the online store?

Checkout supports cash on delivery and online payment through Razorpay or Easebuzz, connected in Settings > Payment providers. You can switch cash on delivery off if you only want prepaid orders. Regular customers you trust can also order on khata (store credit) and pay on their settlement day, with your approval.

## Can I change how my store looks?

Yes. Pick a ready-made theme from the theme store (khatario.com/themes, or Explore themes inside the app) and change it in the store editor: click any part of the page to edit it, drag sections to reorder them, set colours, banners, slideshows, product cards and menus, and preview on desktop, tablet and phone. Changes stay a draft until you publish, earlier versions can be restored, and premium themes are free for every store. Store pages include about, contact, privacy, refund and terms.

## How are orders delivered and returned?

Deliver with your own staff, let customers pick up from the shop, or book a Shiprocket courier from the order (connect Shiprocket once under Settings → Integrations). Customers can ask for a return from their account within the return window you set; you approve it, receive the items back and refund to the original payment, store credit, cash or UPI.

## What else does the store include?

Search with suggestions, category filters and sorting, product reviews, coupons, wishlists, quote requests, abandoned-cart reminders, customer login with OTP, and an enquiries inbox for messages sent from the contact page. Store orders arrive in Khatario and stock stays in sync with your counter sales.
