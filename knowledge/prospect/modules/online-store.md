# Your own online store

## Can I sell online with Khatario?

Yes. Khatario can create an online store for your business at your own address, such as yourshop.khatario.com, using the items and prices already in Khatario. Customers can browse products, add to cart, check out and track their account. The online store is part of the Enterprise plan and is available to try during the free trial.

## How do customers pay on the online store?

Checkout supports cash on delivery and online payment through Razorpay. You can switch cash on delivery off if you only want prepaid orders.

## Can I change how my store looks?

Yes. The store theme studio lets you pick a theme (Studio, Khatario or Premium Grocery), set colours, banners and pages, and preview before publishing. Store pages include about, contact, privacy, refund and terms.

## What else does the store include?

Coupons, product ratings, wishlists, quote requests, customer login with OTP, and an enquiries inbox for messages sent from the contact page. Store orders arrive in Khatario and stock stays in sync with your counter sales.
