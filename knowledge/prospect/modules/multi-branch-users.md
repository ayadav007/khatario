# Team users, roles and branches

## Can my staff use Khatario too?

Yes. Add team users, each with their own login. The number of users depends on your plan (see the plans document): the Free plan has one user, and paid plans allow more.

## Can I control what each staff member sees?

Yes. Roles and permissions decide which modules and actions each user can use, for example a billing clerk who can create invoices but cannot see profit reports or delete bills. Access can also be limited to specific branches and warehouses.

## Does Khatario support multiple branches?

Yes. Business and Enterprise plans support multiple branches, each with its own invoice number series, stock and reports, plus inter-branch transfers and reconciliation. Check the plans document for how many branches each plan allows.

## Can I see who changed what?

Yes. Documents keep a history of changes, deleted records are reported, and period locks stop changes to months you have already closed.
