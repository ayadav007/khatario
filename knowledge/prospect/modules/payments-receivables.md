# Payments, credit (udhaar) and reminders

## How does Khatario help me collect payments?

Khatario shows who owes you money before you open the register: customer balances, receivables ageing (how long each amount has been due) and customer statements. You can send payment reminders on WhatsApp, one at a time or in bulk, and automatic reminders can go out on a schedule on plans that include them.

## Can customers pay online?

Yes. You can connect your own payment gateway account (Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe Business or Instamojo) and add a payment link to invoices and reminders so customers can pay by UPI, card or net banking. Payments made through the link are recorded against the invoice. Your gateway keys are stored encrypted. Payment gateway integration is included in the Enterprise plan and during the free trial.

## Can I record cash, UPI and bank payments?

Yes. Record payments received and payments made with the mode (cash, UPI, card, bank transfer, cheque) and a reference number, and link them to invoices or bills. Receipts can be downloaded as PDF.

## Can I set credit limits for customers?

Yes. Credit-limit alerts warn you when a customer's balance goes above the limit you set, which helps wholesalers manage party-wise credit.

## Does Khatario do bank reconciliation?

Yes. Import your bank statement (CSV or PDF) and match it with the payments recorded in Khatario.
