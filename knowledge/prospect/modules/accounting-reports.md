# Accounting and reports

## Do I need to do accounting entries separately?

No. Invoices, purchases, expenses and payments create the accounting entries automatically. You can still post journal entries for adjustments, enter opening balances when you move from another system, and review any ledger.

## Which accounting features are included?

- Chart of accounts and ledgers for every account and party
- Journal entries, with reusable templates
- Opening balances
- Bank accounts and reconciliation
- TDS categories, certificates and payments
- Financial years and period locks, so closed months cannot be changed
- Fixed assets and depreciation

Ledger accounting is included in the Business and Enterprise plans and during the trial.

## Which reports does Khatario have?

- Profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance and cash flow
- Receivables and payables ageing, and credit risk
- Sales reports: summary, invoice-wise, item-wise, party-wise and tax-wise
- Purchase reports: summary, supplier-wise and tax-wise
- Stock summary, stock valuation and closing stock
- Profit by invoice
- Inter-branch reconciliation
- A custom report builder

Basic reports are on Professional; advanced reports and GST reports are on Business and Enterprise. See the plans document for exact details.

## Can I export reports for my CA?

Yes. Reports and GST returns can be exported (Excel, and JSON for GST) to share with your accountant.
