# Manufacturing, insurance claims and stock audit reports: how-to

## How do I record production (manufacturing)?

Go to Inventory > **Manufacturing**. First save a bill of materials for a batch: the inputs it uses, the output, and any by-products and scrap. Then record a production entry: choose the bill of materials and the godown. Khatario issues the inputs into work in progress at cost, takes scrap and by-products out at their recovery value, and receives the finished goods at the remaining cost, so work in progress clears.

## How do I claim stock lost to theft, fire or flood from insurance?

Go to Inventory > **Insurance Claims**. Record the lost stock and lodge the claim with the insurer. When the insurer decides, mark the claim admitted (with the amount) or rejected, and record the settlement into a bank or cash account when the money comes. A rejected claim moves any admitted amount back to the loss.

## Which stock reports help with a tax audit or a bank loan?

All are under Reports > Stock & Audit:

- **Quantity Details (3CD)**: opening, purchases, sales, returns, closing and shortage or excess for each item, in its own unit, for Form 3CD clause 35.
- **Bank Stock Statement**: the monthly stock statement banks ask for against a cash-credit limit: raw material and finished goods, paid stock, slow-moving and over-90-day stock, eligible debtors less creditors, and the drawing power.
- **Job Work (ITC-04)**: goods sent to job workers on challans, what came back or was lost, and anything past the return deadline, which is then treated as supplied on the challan date.
- **Stock with Parties**: our stock held by others, in transit between godowns, or billed and on the way from suppliers.
