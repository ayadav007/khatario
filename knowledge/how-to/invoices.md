# Invoices: how-to

## How do I create an invoice?

1. Go to Sales > All Invoices and tap New Invoice (or use the + button on the dashboard).
2. Choose the customer, or leave it as a cash sale.
3. Add items by searching the name or scanning a barcode. Price, HSN and GST fill in automatically; change quantity, price or discount if needed.
4. Check the totals, then save. Record the payment now if the customer paid.
5. Print, download the PDF or share on WhatsApp.

## Bill kaise banaye? Invoice kaise banate hain?

Sales > All Invoices mein + New dabao. Customer chuno (ya khali chhodo, cash sale ban jayega). Item ka naam search karo ya barcode scan karo; rate, HSN aur GST apne aap aa jayega. Quantity aur discount (Disc %) badlo, phir Save & Send dabao. Uske baad Print, PDF ya WhatsApp par bhejo.

## How do I give a discount on an invoice?

Enter the discount on each item line in the Disc column (as a percentage; on mobile you can enter Disc % or Disc ₹). The total discount shows in the invoice totals. Use Add Extra Charges for delivery or packing charges and Enable Round Off to round the total.

## How do I record a payment against an invoice?

Open the invoice and use Record payment, or go to Accounting > Payments In and link the payment to one or more invoices. Partly paid invoices show the balance due.

## How do I edit or cancel an invoice?

Open the invoice from Sales > All Invoices and choose Edit or Cancel. Invoices in a locked period (Settings > Period locks) or a month marked as GST filed cannot be changed; use a credit note instead.

## How do I make a credit note for a return?

Go to Sales > Credit Notes, create a new credit note against the original invoice and choose the returned items. Stock comes back and the customer's balance reduces.

## How do I convert a quotation into an invoice?

Open the quotation in Sales > Quotations and convert it to a sales order or invoice. The items and prices carry over.

## How do I set up a recurring invoice?

Create a recurring invoice for monthly clients from the invoices section, choose how often it repeats, and Khatario creates the invoice on schedule.

## How do I change the invoice design?

Settings > Templates & printing lets you choose the template, paper size, colours and which fields print. Custom fields can be added in Settings > Custom fields.
