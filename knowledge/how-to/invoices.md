# Invoices: how-to

## How do I create an invoice?

1. Go to Sales > All Invoices and tap New Invoice (or use the + button on the dashboard).
2. Choose the customer, or leave it as a cash sale.
3. Add items by searching the name or scanning a barcode. Price, HSN and GST fill in automatically; change quantity, price or discount if needed.
4. Check the totals, then save. Record the payment now if the customer paid.
5. Print, download the PDF or share on WhatsApp.

## Bill kaise banaye? Invoice kaise banate hain?

Sales > All Invoices mein + New dabao. Customer chuno (ya khali chhodo, cash sale ban jayega). Item ka naam search karo ya barcode scan karo; rate, HSN aur GST apne aap aa jayega. Quantity aur discount (Disc %) badlo, phir Save & Send dabao. Uske baad Print, PDF ya WhatsApp par bhejo.

## How do I give a discount on an invoice?

Enter the discount on each item line in the Disc column (as a percentage; on mobile you can enter Disc % or Disc ₹). The total discount shows in the invoice totals. Use Add Extra Charges for delivery or packing charges and Enable Round Off to round the total.

## How do I record a payment against an invoice?

Open the invoice and use Record payment, or go to Accounting > Payments In and link the payment to one or more invoices. Partly paid invoices show the balance due.

## How do I edit or cancel an invoice?

Open the invoice from Sales > All Invoices and choose Edit or Cancel. Invoices in a locked period (Settings > Period locks) or a month marked as GST filed cannot be changed; use a credit note instead.

## How do I correct a final invoice (amend)?

Open the final invoice and tap **Amend**. It opens in the full invoice editor, where you can change the date, bill-to and ship-to address, notes, and the PO, e-way bill and dispatch details. The place of supply already saved on the invoice is kept. You can amend until the GSTR-1 that includes the invoice is filed; after that, or in a locked period, issue a credit or debit note instead.

Final bill galat ban gaya to kaise sudhare? Invoice kholkar Amend dabao, galti theek karo aur save karo. GSTR-1 file hone ke baad credit note banana padega.

## How do I make a credit note for a return?

Go to Sales > Credit Notes, create a new credit note, choose **Sales return**, pick the original invoice and the returned items. Stock comes back and the customer's balance reduces.

## How do I give a discount after the sale (post-sale discount credit note)?

For a discount given after the sale, such as a turnover or year-end discount, create a credit note in Sales > Credit Notes and choose **Post-sale discount**. Enter the supply period the discount covers (from and to) and one or more discount lines, each with a description. It is not linked to one invoice and does not move stock. Khatario caps it at what you sold the customer in that period (rate by rate), lists it in GSTR-1 as a credit note without an original invoice, and prints the period on the note.

## How do I use a customer's credit on a new bill?

When a customer has unused credit from a paid credit note (for example a refunded return), the new invoice shows **Customer credit** with the amount available. Apply it and the bill shows "Credit applied", reducing what the customer pays. If the customer paid an advance, the invoice also shows how much was adjusted from it.

## How do I convert a quotation into an invoice?

Open the quotation in Sales > Quotations and convert it to a sales order or invoice. The items and prices carry over.

## How do I set up a recurring invoice?

Create a recurring invoice for monthly clients from the invoices section, choose how often it repeats, and Khatario creates the invoice on schedule.

## How do I change the invoice design?

Settings > Templates & printing lets you choose the template, then Customise it in the designer: colours, paper size, spacing and which fields print. Custom fields can be added in Settings > Custom fields.
