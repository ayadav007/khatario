# Purchases and expenses: how-to

## How do I record a purchase bill?

1. Go to Purchases > All Purchases and tap New Purchase.
2. Choose the supplier and enter the supplier's bill number and date.
3. Add the items, quantity, purchase price and GST.
4. Save. Stock increases and the amount owed to the supplier is recorded.

## How do I add a purchase by scanning the bill?

Use Scan & record in Purchases to upload a photo or PDF of the supplier's bill. Khatario reads the details; check every line before saving.

## How do I return goods to a supplier?

Go to Purchases > Purchase Returns and create a return against the original purchase. Stock reduces and the supplier balance is adjusted.

## How do I record an expense?

Go to Purchases > Expenses and tap Add Expense. An expense is entered as its own bill: choose the supplier, the bill number and date, then add one line per expense item (for example rent, electricity or transport) with its amount, HSN/SAC and GST if any. Suppliers, categories and expense items can be searched or added straight from the dropdown. Under Payment choose Paid by cash, UPI or bank transfer, or "Bill received, not paid yet" to pay it later.

Kharcha kaise likhe? Purchases > Expenses > Add Expense mein bill number, date aur har kharche ki line daalo, phir paid ya baad mein dena hai wo chuno.

## How do I pay a supplier?

Go to Accounting > Payments Out, choose the supplier, enter the amount and mode, and link it to their bills.
