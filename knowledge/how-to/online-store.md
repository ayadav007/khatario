# Online store: how-to

## How do I start my online store?

1. Go to Settings > Sales & billing > Online Store and open the Store setup tab.
2. Enter your **Store URL** (for example my-shop). Your store address becomes my-shop.khatario.com. Use 3–63 lowercase letters, numbers or hyphens.
3. Set **Minimum order (₹)** if you want one, keep **Allow pay on delivery** on if you accept cash on delivery, and choose the **Delivery partner** (Self (own staff) or Shiprocket). Add delivery areas and charges under Delivery zones.
4. Choose which items to sell: in Inventory > Items, turn on **Show in online store** on each item (on the item form or directly in the Items list). Turn on **Featured item (online store)** for up to 6 items to show on the home page.
5. Turn on **Store is live** and tap Save. Use Copy to share the store link with customers on WhatsApp.

The online store is part of the Enterprise plan and can be tried during the free trial. Custom domains (your own .com) are not supported yet; the store runs on your-name.khatario.com.

## Online store kaise banaye?

Settings > Sales & billing > Online Store > Store setup mein Store URL daalo (jaise my-shop, to link banega my-shop.khatario.com). Items mein har product par "Show in online store" on karo. Phir "Store is live" on karke Save karo aur link customers ko WhatsApp par bhejo.

## How do I change the design of my store?

On the Online Store page tap **Themes & Studio**. Pick a theme, then edit sections, colours, logo, banners and pages; preview on Desktop, Tablet or Mobile. Changes save automatically as a draft and customers keep seeing the old design until you tap **Publish**. Use Discard changes to go back to the live design.

Product name, photos, price, MRP and description always come from the item in Inventory > Items. The Pages tab has your About, Contact, Privacy, Refund and Terms pages, and the Promo tab sets an opening offer pop-up (with an optional coupon).

## How do customers pay on my store?

- Cash on delivery, if Allow pay on delivery is on.
- Online payment through Razorpay, if your plan includes the payment gateway and Razorpay is connected in Settings > Payment providers.

Customers verify their mobile number with an OTP before ordering, and can choose delivery or self pickup.

## Where do I see and process online orders?

Go to Settings > Sales & billing > Store Orders. You also get a WhatsApp message on your business number for every new order.
- Orders move through Pending, Confirmed, Ready and Delivered (or Cancelled).
- Confirming an order creates its invoice automatically (cash-on-delivery orders get one when placed). Use the Invoice / thermal print link to print it.
- When marking Ready, choose Customer pickup, Own rider / shop handover, or Book Shiprocket. Unpaid online orders cannot be shipped.
- Use Packing slip, Cash collected (for cash on delivery) and Message on WhatsApp from the order.

## What are store enquiries and how do I reply?

Enquiries are messages customers send from your store's Contact page. Turn on the contact form in Themes & Studio > Contact page > "Show a contact form on the Contact page". New enquiries arrive in Settings > Sales & billing > Store Enquiries, as a Khatario notification and on your business WhatsApp. Open one and tap Reply on WhatsApp, or Mark replied / Archive.
