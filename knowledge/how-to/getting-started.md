# Getting started with Khatario

## What should I set up first?

1. Settings > Business profile: business name, address, state, GST registration type and GSTIN, logo, signature and bank accounts.
2. Settings > Transaction number series: choose how invoice numbers look (prefix and starting number).
3. Settings > Templates & printing: pick an invoice template (A4 or 58mm/80mm thermal) and use Customize to show your signature, bank details and terms, which are hidden by default.
4. Inventory > Items: add your items, or import them from a CSV file.
5. Sales > Customers: add customers now or while billing.
6. Optional: connect WhatsApp (More > Send invoices on WhatsApp) and a payment gateway (Settings > Payment providers).

## How do I enter opening balances?

If you are starting mid-year, enter opening balances so reports start from the right figures: the Opening Balance field when adding each customer (To Receive or To Pay) and supplier, Opening Stock when adding each item, and cash and bank opening balances in Accounting > Chart of Accounts.

## Where do I find a feature I can't see?

Use the search in Settings, or open the More menu. Some features are locked on your current plan and show an upgrade prompt; some need a switch first (for example warehouses and product variants in Settings > Inventory & items > Item defaults); and Connect features need that product added in Settings > Plan & billing > Your products.

## How do I take the guided tour again?

The dashboard offers a short product tour for new users. You can also book a free demo from the Help section in Settings.
