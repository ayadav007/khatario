# Getting started with Khatario

## What should I set up first?

1. Settings > Business profile: business name, address, state, GSTIN, logo, bank details and UPI ID. These print on every invoice.
2. Settings > Transaction number series: choose how invoice numbers look (prefix and starting number).
3. Settings > Templates & printing: pick an invoice template and paper size (A4 or 58mm/80mm thermal).
4. Inventory > Items: add your items, or import them from a CSV file.
5. Sales > Customers: add customers now or while billing.
6. Optional: connect WhatsApp (More > Send invoices on WhatsApp) and a payment gateway (Settings > Payment providers).

## How do I enter opening balances?

If you are starting mid-year, enter what customers owe you, what you owe suppliers, and your cash and bank balances as opening balances, so reports start from the right figures.

## Where do I find a feature I can't see?

Use the search in Settings, or open the More menu. Some features only appear when they are switched on in Settings > UI features, and some are locked on your current plan; locked features show an upgrade prompt.

## How do I take the guided tour again?

The dashboard offers a short product tour for new users. You can also book a free demo from the Help section in Settings.
