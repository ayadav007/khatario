# Supplier Hub and Order list: how-to

## What is the Supplier Hub?

The Supplier Hub connects your Khatario books with the books of the wholesalers and distributors you buy from (and the shops you sell to, if you are a supplier). Once both sides agree to connect, you send orders to each other in Khatario, and the supplier's bill arrives as your purchase in one tap, with no typing or scanning. Open it from Purchases > Supplier Hub. It is included from the Starter plan upwards (see the plans document).

The home page shows **Needs you** (orders, bills and requests waiting for you), then **Buying** (Orders, Incoming bills, New arrivals, My suppliers) and **Selling** (Orders, Shops low on stock, My catalogue, Area report).

## How do I connect with my supplier?

1. In Purchases > Supplier Hub tap **Find suppliers** (or search from the top of the hub). Search by product, brand, supplier name or GSTIN, and filter by category or district.
2. Open the supplier and tap **Ask to connect**. Add a note if you like. Tick **Share stock and sales** if you want the supplier to see when you are running low.
3. Nothing is shared until the supplier accepts. You see the rest of their catalogue once they do.

Supplier hub se kaise jude? Purchases > Supplier Hub > Find suppliers mein apne distributor ko dhundo, Ask to connect dabao. Unke accept karte hi orders aur bill Khatario mein aane lagte hain.

## How do I order from a connected supplier?

Tap **New order** in the Buying panel, choose the supplier and add items. The order shows "Waiting for supplier" until they confirm it. A supplier can also propose an order to you (for example from your low stock); it shows "Waiting for buyer" and you confirm or decline it. After confirming, the supplier makes the bill, the order moves to Billed, then On the way when dispatched, then Received. If only part is confirmed, the rest opens as a linked backorder. Each order has a message thread for both sides.

Prices agreed on the order stay on the bill. If you order in your units (for example pieces) and the supplier sells in packs, the supplier sets how many pieces one pack holds, and Khatario remembers it.

## Can I send an order to a supplier who is not on Khatario?

Yes. The order goes as a private link from your WhatsApp. The supplier can open it and answer without an account, and can join Khatario and connect from the same link.

## How do I receive a supplier's bill?

Bills from connected suppliers arrive in Purchases > Supplier Hub > **Incoming bills** (To receive).

1. Open the bill. Each line is matched to your item; pick your item for any unmatched line, or tap **New item from this line** to create it. Matches are remembered for next time.
2. If something arrived short or damaged, enter it under **Short or damaged**.
3. Tap **Receive and create purchase**. The stock goes in (into the size or colour you ordered) and the purchase is in your books, with the supplier's GST details.

What happens to short and damaged goods is set in Settings > Suppliers directory: keep them for a debit note you record later, or return them to the supplier when you receive the bill.

## What are New arrivals?

Suppliers can publish new collections to every connected buyer. They appear in Purchases > Supplier Hub > **New arrivals**, where you can order straight from them. Use Mute launch alerts if you don't want to be told about new collections.

## How do I list my business as a supplier?

Go to Settings > Suppliers directory. Choose the **Visibility**: listed in the directory, link only (your profile opens from its link but isn't searchable), or hidden. Add a logo and cover photo, featured categories and the items to publish, and accept incoming connection requests there. Fill in My catalogue (photos, sizes and colours with their own price, and a price for each connected buyer if you need one). Orders from buyers then come in under Selling > Orders, and Create bill turns a confirmed order into your sale invoice.

## How do I turn off Supplier Hub WhatsApp messages?

Order, bill and connection updates are sent on WhatsApp. To stop them, go to Settings > Suppliers directory > WhatsApp updates and turn off **Send Supplier Hub updates on WhatsApp**.

## How do I use the Order list?

The Order list (Purchases > Order List, on the web and in the phone app) is for walking the shop and noting what is running out.

1. Search or scan each item, or add it from the **Running low** suggestions. Set the quantity with + and −. Items can have a rack, and the list is sorted by it.
2. Each item's supplier fills in by itself: its default supplier, or else the supplier you last bought it from.
3. Tap **Send orders**. Items without a supplier are listed under "Needs a supplier": tick the ones from the same supplier and **Choose supplier** once. Tick **Remember for next time** to make it their default supplier.
4. Tap **Send all**. Khatario makes one order per supplier: a Supplier Hub order for connected suppliers, and a ready WhatsApp message (Send on WhatsApp) for the rest.

The list is shared by everyone in the business, so staff can add to it too.

Maal khatam ho raha hai to order kaise bheje? Purchases > Order List mein item scan karo ya Running low se jodo, phir Send orders > Send all. Har supplier ko uska order alag se chala jata hai.
