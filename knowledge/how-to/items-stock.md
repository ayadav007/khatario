# Items and stock: how-to

## How do I add an item?

Go to Inventory > Items and tap New Item. Enter the name, unit, selling price, purchase price, HSN/SAC code, GST rate and opening stock. Add a barcode if you scan at the counter. Use More > Tools > HSN/SAC Finder if you don't know the code.

## How do I import items from a file?

In Inventory > Items, use Import, download the CSV template, fill it in and upload it. Khatario shows any rows with errors before importing.

## How do I correct stock?

Use Inventory > Adjustments to add or remove stock with a reason (damage, count difference, and so on).

## How do I move stock between warehouses?

Switch on warehouses, then use Inventory > Stock Transfers to move items from one warehouse to another. Multiple warehouses depend on your plan.

## How do I print barcode labels?

Inventory > Print Labels prints barcode labels for your items, using the designs in Settings > Label templates (where your plan includes it).

## How do I see current stock?

Inventory > Stock Summary shows quantity and value per item; Closing Stock shows stock at a date. Items below their Low Stock Alert quantity show in the dashboard's Low Stock Items card and under the Items list's Low Stock filter.
