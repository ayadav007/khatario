# General settings: how-to

## How do I turn on POS mode for fast counter billing?

Go to Settings > Organization > POS mode (it opens the POS Mode switch in Business profile) and turn it on. New invoices then open in a full-screen checkout: items on the left, payment on the right, with shortcuts F5 (customer phone), F6 (Save & print) and F7 (Save bill). You can park a bill and pick it up later. With a paired Bluetooth printer, tick Auto-print to Bluetooth to print every bill.

POS mode is remembered per device or browser, so turn it on separately on each counter computer or phone. It depends on your plan.

## How do I change colours, dark mode or font?

Settings > General > UI features:
- **Color scheme (this device)**: light or dark mode for your own screen only.
- **Organization portal appearance**: Primary color, Accent color and Font for everyone in your business. Tap Save for organization, or Reset to defaults.

This page does not switch modules on or off; features you don't see are either locked on your plan (they show an upgrade prompt) or belong to a product you haven't added (see Your products).

## How does offline billing work and how do I sync?

Khatario can bill without internet using items, customers and recent invoices saved on the device. Bills made offline get a temporary number and are sent to the server when you are back online, where they receive the final invoice number.

Settings > General > Offline sync shows the connection state and pending bills. Tap Sync now to send pending bills, Delta sync catalog to refresh items and customers, or Full re-download if the offline data looks wrong. The Offline → final invoice numbers list shows which offline bill became which invoice. Anything under Manual review required (for example a GST conflict) should be checked by your accountant.

## What is the suppliers directory?

Settings > Organization > Suppliers directory lets other Khatario businesses find you as a supplier in the Suppliers Hub and send connection requests.
1. Choose Visibility: Hidden (default), Link only, or Directory (searchable in the Suppliers Hub).
2. Write a Profile summary (what you supply) and Featured categories.
3. Publish up to 20 items for buyers to see (prices show as "on request").
4. Tap Save Changes. When a buyer sends a connection request, it appears under Incoming connection requests; tap Accept to link your business as their supplier, or Decline.

## How do I add another Khatario product like HR or Connect?

Settings > Plan & billing > Your products shows Billing, HR and Connect. Tap Add next to a product to choose its plan or start a trial. If you use more than one, Set as primary decides which home screen opens after login. Each product has its own plan; admin logins are shared.

## Where can I find help, guides or the product tour?

Settings > Help > Help & support lets you restart the product tour (menu, sidebar or business profile). Settings > Help > How-to guides has step-by-step guides for setup, invoices, purchases, stock, accounts, GST returns and WhatsApp. For anything else, email help@khatario.com or ask this assistant.
