# Payments and reminders: how-to

## How do I see who owes me money?

Reports > Receivables Aging lists every customer's balance by how long it has been due. Open a customer in Sales > Customers to see their statement.

## How do I send a payment reminder on WhatsApp?

Link your WhatsApp number by QR code first (Settings > WhatsApp > Connection). Then:
- One customer (every plan): Sales > Customers, tap the green WhatsApp icon next to a customer who owes you money.
- Many invoices at once (Starter plan and above): Sales > Payment Reminders.
- Automatic reminders before the due date and while overdue (Starter plan and above): Settings > WhatsApp > Reminders.

All of these go out from your QR-linked number. You do not need Khatario Connect for reminders.

## How do I add a payment link to invoices?

Connect your gateway account (Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe Business or Instamojo) in Settings > Payment providers. Invoices and reminders can then include a link for the customer to pay online, and the payment is recorded automatically. Payment gateway integration is part of the Enterprise plan and the free trial.

## How do I record a payment I received in cash or UPI?

Go to Accounting > Payments In, choose the customer, amount, mode and reference, and link it to the invoices it pays.

## How do I match my bank statement?

Upload the statement in Accounting > Bank import (CSV or PDF), then match entries in Bank reconciliation.
