# Settings: how-to

## How do I add a staff user?

1. Go to Settings > Users & access > User management and switch on User Roles & Permissions (only needed once; adding users fails while it is off).
2. Go to Settings > Users & access > Manage users and tap Add New User.
3. Enter User Name, Phone Number, optional Email, a Password you choose for them, and the User Role (Sales, Accountant, Inventory Manager or a custom role). If you have more than one branch, pick Assign to Branch.
4. Tap Create User and share the phone number and password with them. They log in on the Khatario login page with that phone number and password.

The number of users depends on your plan; when you reach the limit Khatario shows an upgrade prompt. A phone number can belong to only one Khatario user.

## Staff ko access kaise de? Naya user kaise banaye?

Settings > Users & access > User management mein User Roles & Permissions on karo. Phir Manage users > Add New User mein naam, mobile number, password aur role (Sales, Accountant, Inventory Manager) chuno aur Create User dabao. Staff wahi mobile number aur password se login karega.

## How do I remove or block a staff member?

In Settings > Users & access > Manage users, tap Deactivate on the user to block their login (Activate brings them back), or Delete to remove them permanently. Tap Edit to change their role, branch or password. The Primary admin cannot be deactivated or deleted.

## How do I control what each staff member can see or do?

Go to Settings > Users & access > Manage roles. Built-in roles are Primary Admin (full access), Sales (invoices, customers, payments), Accountant (sales, purchases, payments and reports) and Inventory Manager (purchases, suppliers, items, warehouses). To make your own, tap Create Role, enter a Role Name, then select the role and tick the permissions (view, add, modify, delete, share) for each module; every tick saves immediately. Primary Admin cannot be changed.

## How do I limit a user to one branch?

Settings > Users & access > User branches > Assign User: choose the user and branch and tick Create Transactions, View Reports and/or Manage Inventory. Users can only create transactions and see reports for the branches they are assigned to.

## How do I see who changed what?

Settings > Users & access > Activity logs lists the latest 100 actions (created, updated or deleted invoices, purchases, customers, suppliers, users, roles and settings) with the user, time and IP address. Filter by module or by user.

## How do I upgrade or change my plan?

Go to Settings > Plan & billing, choose the plan and monthly or yearly billing, and pay online through Razorpay.

## How do I take a backup?

Go to Settings > Backup & restore. Take a backup now, schedule automatic backups, or connect Google Drive. Restoring can replace all data or merge.

## How do I connect a Bluetooth thermal printer?

Go to Settings > Print & devices, turn on the printer, pair it and choose 58mm or 80mm paper. Then choose a thermal template in Settings > Templates & printing. POS mode can print automatically after each bill.

## How do I lock a past period?

Settings > Period locks stops anyone from changing entries on or before a date, for example after your CA closes the books.

## How do I change my password?

Use Forgot password on the login page, or change it from your profile.
