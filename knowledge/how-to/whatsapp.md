# WhatsApp: how-to

## How do I connect my WhatsApp number?

1. Open Settings > WhatsApp (also in the sidebar: WhatsApp, or Connect > WhatsApp Settings).
2. On the Connection tab, tap Connect WhatsApp to show a QR code.
3. On your phone, open WhatsApp > Linked devices > Link a device, and scan the QR code.
4. Once it shows Connected, Khatario can send invoices and reminders from your number.

Keep your phone online occasionally so the link stays active. If it disconnects, scan the QR code again.

The QR link works on every plan, including Free. To send through the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API from your own WhatsApp Business Account instead, you need the Khatario Connect plan; then fill in the Meta Cloud API section on the same tab. If Connect lapses, Khatario goes back to your QR link and keeps your Meta details for when you renew.

## Where do I find each WhatsApp setting?

Settings > WhatsApp has these tabs, in setup order:

- Connection: your number by QR code, and the Meta Cloud API (Connect).
- Reminders: automatic payment reminders (Starter plan and above) and updates sent to you.

Under the Connect heading (each shows a lock until you have the Connect plan):

- Team & inbox: WhatsApp agents, how chats are shared, and saved replies.
- AI agent: the assistant that answers customer chats.
- Templates: which approved Meta template each message uses (Cloud API only).
- Shop: take orders on WhatsApp.

## How do I send an invoice on WhatsApp?

Open the invoice and tap the WhatsApp button. The customer's mobile number must be saved on the customer. This works on every plan with a QR-linked number.

## How do I send payment reminders now?

- One customer (every plan): in Sales > Customers, tap the green WhatsApp icon next to a customer who owes you money (their mobile number must be saved).
- Many invoices at once (Starter plan and above): open Sales > Payment Reminders (or Settings > WhatsApp > Reminders > Send reminders), pick the invoices and send. The History tab shows what was sent and whether it was delivered.

These go out from your QR-linked number; you do not need Connect.

## How do I set up automatic payment reminders?

Automatic reminders are included from the Starter plan upwards. Open Settings > WhatsApp > Reminders, turn on the payment-due reminder (how many days before the due date) and the overdue reminder (how often to repeat), and save. They are sent from your QR-linked number, so keep it connected; with Connect and the Cloud API set up they go through the official API instead. On the Free plan reminders are sent by hand only.

## Why did my WhatsApp message fail?

Common reasons: the number is not connected or was logged out, the customer's number is wrong or not on WhatsApp, or you have reached your plan's daily message limit. Check the Connection tab and try again.

## How do I use the WhatsApp inbox and bot?

These need the Khatario Connect plan. Connect > Chats > Conversations shows all chats; set up guided auto-replies in Connect > Chats > Flows or the AI agent in Settings > WhatsApp > AI agent, and review WhatsApp orders in Connect > Chats > Order Verification. Campaigns and one-off messages are under Connect > Send, and contacts, groups and unsubscribes under Connect > Audience.
