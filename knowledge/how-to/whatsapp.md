# WhatsApp: how-to

## How do I connect my WhatsApp number?

1. Open Settings > WhatsApp (also in the sidebar: WhatsApp, or Connect > WhatsApp Settings).
2. On the Connection tab, tap Connect WhatsApp to show a QR code.
3. On your phone, open WhatsApp > Linked devices > Link a device, and scan the QR code.
4. Once it shows Connected, Khatario can send invoices and reminders from your number.

Keep your phone online occasionally so the link stays active. If it disconnects, scan the QR code again.

Businesses with an approved Meta number can instead fill in the Meta Cloud API section on the same tab.

## Where do I find each WhatsApp setting?

Settings > WhatsApp has these tabs:

- Connection: your number, QR code and Meta Cloud API.
- Templates: which approved Meta template each message uses (Cloud API only).
- Notifications: automatic payment reminders and updates sent to you.
- Inbox & team: how chats are assigned and saved replies (Connect).
- AI agent: the assistant that answers customer chats.
- Shop: take orders on WhatsApp.

## How do I send an invoice on WhatsApp?

Open the invoice and tap the WhatsApp button. The customer's mobile number must be saved on the customer.

## How do I send payment reminders now?

With the WhatsApp Bot add-on, open Connect > Payment Reminders to send reminders for unpaid invoices and see the history. Automatic reminder timing is under Settings > WhatsApp > Notifications.

## Why did my WhatsApp message fail?

Common reasons: the number is not connected or was logged out, the customer's number is wrong or not on WhatsApp, or you have reached your plan's daily message limit. Check the Connection tab and try again.

## How do I use the WhatsApp inbox and bot?

With the WhatsApp Bot add-on, Connect > Conversations shows all chats; set auto-replies in Connect > Bot Rules or set up the AI agent in Settings > WhatsApp > AI agent, and review WhatsApp orders in Connect > Order Verification.
