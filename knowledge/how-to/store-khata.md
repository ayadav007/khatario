# Store khata (order now, pay monthly): how-to

## What is store khata?

Store khata lets regular customers you trust order from your online store on credit and pay on their settlement day each month, like the khata at the counter. The khata is the customer's whole balance with you, so their counter bills on credit are included too.

Store se udhaar par order kaise de? Customer kholkar Store khata mein Open khata dabao, settlement day aur limit set karo. Phir wo store se khata par order kar sakta hai.

## How do I open a khata for a customer?

Open the customer in Sales > Customers (the full page or the side panel) and find the **Store khata** card. Tap **Open khata**, choose the **Settlement day** (a day from 1 to 28, or the last day of the month) and a **Credit limit** (leave blank for no limit). You can also set this customer's own rule for when they go over the limit or have overdue bills, or keep the business rule.

A shopper can also tap **Request khata** in their store account. You get an alert, and the request shows on the customer's Store khata card, where you open the khata or dismiss the request.

To stop new khata orders, close the khata. What the customer owes stays on their ledger.

## How do I set the khata rules for my store?

Go to Settings > Sales & billing > Online Store and find **Khata (order now, pay monthly)**:
- **Order goes over the credit limit** and **Customer has overdue bills**: Block the order, Allow and tell me, or Ask me to approve.
- **Grace period**: days after the settlement day before a bill counts as overdue.
- **WhatsApp statement**: sent on each customer's settlement day, plus overdue reminders (set **Overdue reminder every** how many days).
- **Part payment online**: lets customers pay some of their khata from the store.

## How do I approve a khata order?

When a rule asks for your approval, the order is marked "Khata · approve" in Settings > Sales & billing > Store Orders (filter **Khata to approve**). Approve it to bill it on the customer's khata, or reject it to cancel it and ask them to pay another way. An order waiting for approval can't be marked Ready.

For pickup orders on khata, the "ready to collect" WhatsApp tells the customer there is nothing to pay at pickup and that the amount is added to their khata with its due date.

## How do customers see and pay their khata?

Customers sign in to your store with their mobile number (OTP) and open **My khata** to see their balance, statement, orders and payments. If you have connected a payment gateway they can pay online; with only a UPI ID they get a Pay by UPI button and you confirm the payment yourself; otherwise it says to pay at the shop. The WhatsApp statement links to this page.

## How do I record a khata payment at the counter?

On the customer's Store khata card, record the payment with its amount, date and how they paid (cash, UPI, bank transfer or cheque). It is set against the oldest bills first. You can also send the statement on WhatsApp from the card. A payment larger than the open bills is refused; record the extra as an advance instead.
