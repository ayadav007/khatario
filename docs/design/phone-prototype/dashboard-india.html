Notes

  • Orange is only used for "New bill". Green means money in and red means money out, everywhere.
  • Amounts stay in Latin digits and the Indian grouping (₹1,24,500) in every language. That is what most Indian billing apps do.
  • Party and item names are data, so they are not translated.
  • "Remind" would open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent without the owner tapping send. The prototype only marks it as sent.
  • The UPI / Cash / Bank split needs collection grouped by payment mode from the overview API. That is the one new number here.
  • Translations are a first pass and should be checked by a native speaker before shipping.
