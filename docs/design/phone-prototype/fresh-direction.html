Rules

  • Spacing on a 4px grid. Screen gutter is 16px.
  • Radius 10 for chips and inputs, 16 for cards, 24 for sheets.
  • Cards have no borders, only a soft shadow. A hairline separates rows inside a card.
  • Each screen has one primary action, either a floating button or a bottom bar.
  • Touch targets are at least 44px.
  • Pickers, filters and menus open as bottom sheets, not web dropdowns.
  • Status is shown as a coloured dot and a word. No outlined badges.
