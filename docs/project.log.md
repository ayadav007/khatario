## 2026-10-28: Party pricing brought up to Tally/Busy/Vyapar
- **Asked:** "please complete all the features" from the party pricing review: price levels, quantity slabs, dates, history, discount %, last rate charged, supplier-wise purchase rates, per-unit prices, below-cost warning, sales orders and challans, one buyer price list.
- **Decided:** one server resolver (`lib/pricing/resolve-price.ts`, `POST /api/pricing/resolve`) picks the price for invoices, sales orders and challans: customer price, then the customer's price level (slabs by min qty, then dates), then the item's own price. Discount comes from the customer's item rule, else a category discount, else the level rate, and goes in the line's discount column (challans have none, so it is taken off the rate). Slabs and dates live on price levels; customer prices get dates and discount % but no slabs. A line whose price or discount was typed, loaded from a saved document or agreed on a hub order is never repriced. History is written by the APIs into `price_change_log` (not a trigger, so it records the user). Supplier Hub buyer prices moved into `party_item_prices` for the linked customer; `supplier_buyer_prices` is kept but no longer read. The supplier rate is the last price on a saved purchase bill from that supplier, not a new table. Below-cost compares the pre-GST net price with the item's purchase price.
- **Not done, on purpose:** per-unit prices, because invoice lines cannot be sold in an alternate unit yet (unit is read-only, stock is not converted); that needs selling in alternate units first. Bills made without a screen (recurring, hub conversion, AI agent) keep the prices they carry and are not repriced.
- **Shipped:** migration `434_price_levels_and_party_pricing.sql` (applied to `kh_phase2_test` only); APIs `pricing/resolve`, `pricing/supplier-rates`, `pricing/levels`, `pricing/levels/[id]/rates`, `pricing/party-category`, `pricing/history`, reworked `pricing/party-item` and `suppliers/hub/buyer-prices`; pages `/pricing/levels` (new) and `/pricing/party-item` (discount, dates, level, category discounts, history); invoice, sales order, challan and purchase screens; `usePartyPricing` hook. ## 2026-10-28: Item edits keep their variants
- **Asked:** stop the item PATCH from deleting and re-inserting variants, which gave every variant a new id, reset its stock to opening stock and cut links from invoice, purchase and stock-movement lines.
- **Decided:** the "Handle Variants Update" block in `app/api/items/[id]/route.ts` now syncs in one transaction: a variant sent with an id that belongs to this item is updated in place, any other is inserted (an id from another item is ignored, never trusted), and only this item's variants left out of the edit are deleted. Stock on hand keeps every movement; a change to an existing variant's opening stock shifts it by the difference (opening 10 → 12 moves current 7 → 9). With ids stable, the carry-over of `party_item_prices` by variant name was removed. The web item form already sends each variant's id; the phone app does not send variants on edit, so it is unaffected. The bundle branch still deletes variants, since a bundle cannot have any.
- **Shipped:** `app/api/items/[id]/route.ts`, new `tests/db/item-variant-sync.db.test.ts` (3 pass on `kh_phase2_test`; `party-item-price-variants` still 4 pass), `npx tsc --noEmit` clean. Nothing committed; not checked in the browser.
- **Open:** swapping barcodes between two variants in one edit nulls the second one (the per-item duplicate check sees the first variant's old barcode), same as any duplicate.
