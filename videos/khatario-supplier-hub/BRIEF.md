## Intent

An explainer for Khatario customers showing how the Supplier Hub fixes the daily
pain between a retailer and a wholesaler: stock running out, orders on the phone,
typing supplier bills by hand, cartons vs pieces mismatch, short deliveries
forgotten. It follows the exact flow tested on staging on 17 Oct 2026: connect →
low-stock suggested order → confirm → supplier's bill lands as your purchase
(3 boxes become 36 packs) → mark 4 short → debit note ready → new collection
launched and ordered in sets. Friendly, shopkeeper-to-shopkeeper tone.

## Customizations

- Hinglish voiceover (warm female voice) and Hinglish on-screen words.
- Two deliverables: 16:9 (1920x1080) for YouTube / website, and a 9:16
  (1080x1920) version for WhatsApp Status and Instagram Reels.
- Minimal flat illustration, as in the approved samples: cream background
  #F7F4EE, deep green ink #063F35, one orange accent #F58220, soft green
  #EEF2F1, hairline #E4DDD0. Simple shapes, no faces, very little text.
