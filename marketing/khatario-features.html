What the party actually receives.

The same sale can leave as a GST invoice, a Tally-style bill, a challan, a credit note, or a thermal slip. These are Khatario’s own templates.

The rate

The wholesaler does not pay the shelf price.

Party pricing stores one rate per customer per item, to the paisa. On the invoice, that rate is fetched before anyone types it. An empty cell falls back to the item price.

Offers sit on top: a percent, a flat amount, a discount after the bill crosses a threshold, or buy-X-get-Y. They are calculated in paise. Free units are added to the quantity, so GST stays on what was paid and stock falls by what left the shop. Several offers can apply in order, or only the highest priority one.

Party pricing, offers, POS, and barcode labels start on Growth.

The month

Sales, collection, purchases, profit.

The dashboard adds final invoices, what was collected, purchases, and profit for the range you pick. A proforma is not a sale. A staff login is limited to the branches they can open.

If an item’s default supplier is another Khatario business, and stock is at or below the minimum, it becomes a reorder request. They confirm a quantity. You cannot purchase more than they confirmed. If they are short, they can pass the same request to their supplier.

Dead stock is quantity still on hand with no final sale in 90 days.

Wholesalers

See which district is selling your goods, and which is only stocking them.

When your dealers are also on Khatario and they have allowed you to see their stock, the supplier dashboard draws them on a map of India. You can look at the last 30 days, the last 90, or this financial year, by state, city, or PIN code.

Two numbers sit on every area. Bought from you is their posted purchase bills. Sold onward is their posted sales of items they buy from you, items where you are the default supplier, or items you set a stock threshold on. A pivot shows which of your items is moving in which state. A ranking shows who is carrying the business.

  • Doing wellThey are buying from you and selling onward.
  • WatchGoods went in and less than a quarter of that value has sold through, or the area is only middling next to your other dealers. The stock is not earning its place.
  • Needs attentionAmong the dealers who are billing, this area is in the bottom third.
  • No movementNo bills in the period. The map only includes customers who granted this access. A dealer who has not linked you does not appear.

The same screen lists low-stock alerts by dealer: item, quantity on hand, the threshold you agreed, and their phone, city, and PIN. You can raise a refill from that list instead of waiting for them to call.

This is the wholesaler’s view. A retail shop that only buys, and does not supply other Khatario businesses, will not have dealers on this map.

Vendors

Your supplier is a ledger, a bill, and a person you can ask for stock.

Each supplier has a balance, a statement, and an ageing of what you owe. Purchase orders become purchases when the goods arrive. Finalising the bill adds stock and posts the payable. A return takes both back. A photo or PDF of their invoice is matched to the supplier on GSTIN or exact name, and each line is matched by name and HSN, before you save it.

If that supplier also uses Khatario and you have linked them, they are more than a name in a list. When your stock is at or below the minimum, Khatario suggests a request. They confirm a quantity, and you cannot purchase more than they confirmed. If they do not have it, they can pass the same request to their supplier. A shop, a distributor, and a manufacturer can sit on one thread, each seeing only their side.

Credit risk is shown for suppliers as well as customers: how much of the limit is already used, and whether it is healthy, on watch, or critical.

The message

The bill leaves on your WhatsApp.

From the invoice you send the PDF, copy a link they can open without an account, or send it on WhatsApp from the number you connected. Every billing plan can do this. The daily cap is 20 on Free, 200 on Growth, and 500 on Business.

The assistant, on Connect, may answer only from your items and prices. An order line has to be an item name as stored. A payment screenshot is accepted only when the amount matches and the read is confident. Anything else stays on review until a person decides.

Sending a bill is on every billing plan. The assistant, the inbox, and campaigns are Connect: ₹1,499 a month, ₹14,388 a year, or ₹34,999 for three years. Five users, 1,000 messages a day, 500 AI replies a month.

The assistant

It sells from your list. It is not allowed to invent a price.

The WhatsApp assistant replies in the language the customer wrote: English, Hindi, or Hinglish. It may use only the items, prices, and policies you gave it. If the answer is not there, it says it will check with the team. It does not make up a product or an offer.

An order is created only when the item name matches the catalogue. Delivery charges stay in the message. They are not smuggled in as a product line. It remembers the customer’s name and what they asked for in that chat, and it will not open a second order for one that already exists. If they ask for a person, the chat is handed to your staff. Each conversation is marked hot, warm, cold, or not interested, so the team can see who is ready to buy before they reply.

A payment screenshot is read for the amount, the UPI id, and the transaction reference. It is marked verified only when it is a payment image, the amount matches the order, and the read is confident. Otherwise it stays on review until someone accepts or rejects it.

Campaigns

One message to a list, with a count of who received it.

A campaign is a text, an image, or a message with buttons, sent to a contact group. You can pause it while it is running. The screen shows how many were sent, how many failed, and how many are still waiting. People who have unsubscribed are kept off the next send. Message only customers who agreed to hear from you.

Your store

The website sells the stock the counter already counts.

On Business, the same items become a shop at an address like yourshop.khatario.com. Customers browse, add to cart, and pay by Razorpay or cash on delivery. You can turn cash on delivery off. The order lands in Khatario, and a sale on the website moves the same quantity as a sale on the counter.

The theme studio has two looks you can publish: Studio, and Premium Grocery. You set colours, banners, and the pages a shop needs — about, contact, privacy, refunds, terms — and preview before it goes live. Coupons, ratings, wishlists, quote requests, and customer login with a one-time code are included. Messages from the contact page sit in an enquiries inbox.

There is no second catalogue to maintain. That is the only reason a billing product should offer a store.

Your CA

A file, not your GST password.

GSTR-1 is prepared from final bills. You export Excel, the portal’s offline workbook, or JSON. You or your CA upload it. Khatario does not log into the GST portal.
